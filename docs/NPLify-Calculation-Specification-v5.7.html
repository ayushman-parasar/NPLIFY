Numbered with the ERD edition it is built against. Supersedes Draft v1.0 (ERD v4.0).

Status: Built against ERD & Data Model Draft v5.7 (decisions D1–D46) and the Ledger Posting Design v5.7. It states every calculation the engine performs as a precise formula, in the order it is performed, with the rounding rule at each step, and gives acceptance vectors of two kinds: the Ledger Posting Design's worked deals, produced by the same pricing function this document imports, and NPL's own figures from its fee table, recomputed here and compared. The engine passes when it reproduces every vector.
What changed since v1.0: fee parts are percentages of the principal at the basis rate and never compound (NPL, 11 October); a target-out quote applies the fee as a markup on the target (NPL's sheets); the rate basis is market or agent_rate with the partner's cost inside its rate or stated separately; tiers, the customer-facing figures, the rounding menu, cash rounding, same-currency pairs, the markup-share base, rebates, the four variance kinds, the forex true-up on invoice instalments, earnings retained and offset between partners, loss splits, and the reseller arithmetic are all specified (D20–D46, with the same-day amendment of D46: views over postings, the two-invoice links, FX_RESULT, the target-out rule).

1 · Conventions

  • Rates are stored normalised to the pair's quote_direction (out units per 1 in unit unless the pair says in_per_out) at CURRENCY_PAIR.rate_precision. A partner's rate is stored as quoted (PARTNER_RATE_VERSION.quoted_rate, quoted_direction) and the normalised rate is derived: equal to the quote when the directions agree, 1 ÷ quote at the pair's precision when they differ (D28).
  • Amounts are exact decimals; fiat and USDT amounts are computed to 2 decimals (BTC-like assets to 8) with round half up, then rounded as the pair's option says (§9). Every intermediate figure in this document is computed, not typed.
  • Percentages are stored as fractions (1.4 % = 0.014). sender_pays_pct + receiver_pays_pct = FEE_STRUCTURE.pct.
  • Precision of a check: an acceptance vector matches when the computed figure and NPL's figure agree to the cent, or to the unit where NPL's sheet rounds to the unit.

2 · Rate basis, derived rates and the breach check

FEE_STRUCTURE.rate_basis says which rate the sender is priced on: market (the fee is disclosed; the partner's cost comes out of NPL's fee parts — Evo, the reseller projects) or agent_rate (a calculated rate, breakdown not disclosed; NPL keeps the whole fee and the partner's cost sits on top — BF via Ali, D22). The partner's own pricing is PARTNER_PAIR.partner_pricing: fee_in_rate (its cost is inside its rate, never posted; Ali, assumed 1 %) or fee_on_market (it converts at market and states partner_fee_pct separately; Jeton 1.25 %, Aquanow 0.80 %).

basis_rate = market_rate if rate_basis = market = partner_rate_version.rate if rate_basis = agent_rate rate = quoted_rate if quoted_direction = pair.quote_direction = round(1 ÷ quoted_rate, rate_precision) otherwise (D28) spread = 1 − partner_rate ÷ market_rate (what the partner keeps against market, fee_in_rate) breach = spread > avg_2w_spread × (1 + variance_threshold_rel) → the pair freezes until Finance decides

3 · Fee parts on the principal

The sender's part and the receiver's part are each a percentage of the principal at the basis rate. They are never compounded on each other (NPL, 11 October: 300,000 USDT × 0.9000 − 2 % = 264,600, not 264,622.68).

principal = amount_in × basis_rate (fixed_in) fee_sender_part = principal × sender_pays_pct fee_receiver_part = principal × receiver_pays_pct sender_rate = basis_rate × (1 − sender_pays_pct) (the rate the sender is given) gross_out = amount_in × sender_rate = principal − fee_sender_part client_net = gross_out − fee_receiver_part = principal × (1 − sender_pays_pct − receiver_pays_pct)
  • 10,000 USDT × market 0.9000 = 9,000.00 EUR (the principal at the basis rate)
  • sender part = 9,000 × 1.40 % = 126.00; receiver part = 9,000 × 0.60 % = 54.00
  • sender rate = 0.9000 × (1 − 1.40 %) = 0.88740; gross out = 10,000 × 0.88740 = 8,874.00
  • client net = 9,000 × (1 − 2.00 %) = 8,820.00 = gross out − receiver part
Confirmed rule (NPL, 11 October). Both parts are on the principal. NPL's fee sheet for Evo prints the receiver side as "EUR 25,704 − 0.6 % = 25,550", which takes the receiver's part from the gross out; on the principal it is 25,547.88, a difference of 0.6 % × 1.4 % of the principal (about 0.008 %). The sheet's receiver line is to be brought in line with the rule; the engine follows the rule.

4 · Fixed and variable fee modes; the minimum-margin check

In fixed mode pct is applied as above. In variable mode the fee is whatever leaves NPL its minimum margin after the partner's cost, within a floor and a cap: this is the check that decides whether 2 % is enough when the agent rate is high.

required_pct = min_margin_pct + spread if rate_basis = market (the partner's cost comes out of the fee) = min_margin_pct if rate_basis = agent_rate fee_pct = min(max_markup_pct, max(min_markup_pct, required_pct)) below_margin = fee_pct < required_pct → warn, never refuse a quote; Finance may ask the partner for a better rate or recommend a fee above the cap (raise_fee_cap, Management approves, D41)

5 · Fee tiers by monthly volume (D21)

tier = the FEE_TIER of the structure with the highest min_monthly_volume ≤ PROJECT_MONTH_VOLUME at quote portion_1 = max(0, threshold_of_next_tier − PROJECT_MONTH_VOLUME) capped at amount_in, priced at tier.pct portion_2 = amount_in − portion_1 priced at next_tier.pct fee_parts = Σ portions × basis_rate × pct (sender and receiver parts in the structure's proportion) — the blended sum

GDC: 2.5 % up to USD 500,000 a month, 2.25 % above; a leg that takes the month past the threshold is split into two DEAL_FEE_TIER portions.

6 · Amount modes: fixed_in and target_out

A collect-first deal prices what arrived (fixed_in, §3). A quote-first deal for a given out amount (target_out: an invoice, a GDC USD amount, a reseller's EUR) applies the fee as a markup on the target, which is how NPL's sheets print it (EUR 50,000 × 1.01 ÷ rate; USD 84,482 + 2.5 %).

amount_in = target_out × (1 + sender_pays_pct) ÷ basis_rate (then cash-rounded if collected in cash, §9) sender_rate = basis_rate ÷ (1 + sender_pays_pct) (printed as target ÷ amount_in) client_net = target_out × (1 − receiver_pays_pct) + cash_rounding_surplus fee_sender_part = amount_in × basis_rate − (target_out + cash_rounding_surplus)

Note: × (1 + s) and ÷ (1 − s) differ by s² (0.01 % at a 1 % fee). NPL's convention, × (1 + s), is adopted; the fixed_in formula keeps × (1 − s). The cash-rounding surplus is the client's (in NPL-GR, NPL-GR's).

  • NPL-GR: invoice 9,450 EUR ÷ 0.8708 = 10,852.09 USDT to deliver
  • amount_in (exact) = 10,852.09 × (1 + 2 %) × 32.50 THB per USDT = 359,746.78 THB; collected as cash, rounded up to the 1,000 = 360,000 THB
  • client net = 360,000 ÷ 32.50 × (1 − 2 %)… no: in target mode the fee is on the target, so client net = target + the cash-rounding surplus = 10,859.73 USDT (7.63 surplus); sender part = amount_in × market − client net = 217.19
  • Ali converts at 1 ÷ 32.83: delivered 10,966.15; NPL earnings = 10,966.15 − 10,859.73 = 106.42 = the 2 % markup less Ali's 1 %

7 · Customer-facing figures (D22)

customer_facing_fee_pct = sender_pays_pct + receiver_pays_pct if customer_facing_fee_basis = actual = sender_pays_pct on the agent rate if sender_pays_on_agent_rate = FEE_STRUCTURE.customer_facing_fee_pct if fixed (BF via Ali prints 1 %) customer_facing_rate = sender_rate ÷ (1 − customer_facing_fee_pct) — the "market rate" the quote prints, derived, never typed

8 · What the partner delivers, and NPL's earnings

fee_in_rate: delivered = amount_in × partner_rate; partner_cost (inferred) = amount_in × (market − partner_rate), information only fee_on_market: converted = amount_in × market_rate; partner_cost = converted × partner_fee_pct; delivered = converted − partner_cost earnings = delivered − client_net (NPL's margin: EARN_GROSS − EXP_PARTNER) EARN_GROSS = converted − client_net (fee_on_market: the full fee parts, the partner's cost booked apart as EXP_PARTNER; fee_in_rate: the fee parts less the partner's hidden cost — equal to earnings) retained_in = earnings_in_out_currency ÷ partner_rate when the margin is kept in the collection currency (CONVERSION.residual_in, D46) converted_in = amount_in − retained_in − offset_in (what the partner actually converts, §13)
  • Ali (fee_in_rate) converts at 0.8910: delivered = 10,000 × 0.8910 = 8,910.00; NPL earnings = 8,910.00 − 8,820.00 = 90.00; Ali's inferred cost = 10,000 × (0.9000 − 0.8910) = 90.00, information only
  • Jeton (fee_on_market 1.25 %) converts at market: 9,000.00; partner cost = 9,000.00 × 1.25 % = 112.50; delivered 8,887.50; fee charged 180.00 (EARN_GROSS); NPL earnings = 8,887.50 − 8,820.00 = 67.50

9 · Rounding and precision (D25, D46)

payout: amount_rounding = exact | truncate_unit | nearest_unit | round_up_unit | nearest_n | round_up_n (rounding_unit) payable = round(client_net, option); residual = client_net − payable (signed) residual > 0 → stays client money in DUE; residual < 0 → NPL's cost, EXP_ROUNDING, out of its margin cash in: cash_rounding = truncate_n | nearest_n | round_up_n (cash_rounding_unit: AED 100, USD cash 10, THB 1,000) — the collection side; a cash payout rounds by the pair's amount_rounding (round_up_n 1,000 for THB hand-overs) amount_in = round(exact amount_in, option); surplus = (amount_in − exact) × basis_rate × (1 − fee) → the client's rates: rate_precision per pair (6 for EUR/USDT, 8 for BTC); intermediate amounts 2 dp (8 for BTC), round half up

Bank transfers go out exact, cents included; rounding to a unit applies where the pair says so, in practice to cash hand-overs (NPL, 11 October). USDT paid by reseller customers is rounded up.

  • THB payout in cash: client net 95,844.00 THB, cash_rounding round_up_n 1,000 → 96,000.00; residual = 95,844.00 − 96,000.00 = -156.00 (negative: NPL's cost, EXP_ROUNDING); NPL's pool gets 966.00 -156.00 = 810.00

basis_rate = partner_rate = 1; fee parts and client_net as in §3; earnings = fee_sender_part + fee_receiver_part

Evo EUR → EUR: 2.8 %, sender 1.4 % and receiver 1.4 %: 5,000 EUR → client net 4,860.00, NPL 140.00.

11 · Markup shares and rebates (D26, D27, D46)

share base (share_of_net_markup) = fee_sender_part + fee_receiver_part − partner_cost (rebates never in the base) share_i = base × pct_i; Σ pct_i ≤ 100 %; accrued and posted with the conversion npl_pays: SHARE_PAYABLE to the party, off EARN_GROSS party_retains: the party holds the funds; NPL's remainder = base − Σ shares → SHARE_RECEIVABLE share_of_earnings: share = earnings × pct; fixed_pct: priced into the sender rate rebate = amount_in × pct (basis pct_of_amount, the only basis; Jeton 0.4 %, Aquanow 0.3 %, Novi 0.3 %), in PARTNER_REBATE_RULE.payout_currency accrued with the conversion → REBATE_RECEIVABLE / EARN_REBATE; the statement clears it, a difference trues up EARN_REBATE
  • Raeen sub-account at Aquanow: 10,000 × 0.9000 = 9,000; sender pays 4 % = 360.00; client net 8,640.00; Aquanow 0.80 % of 9,000 = 72.00; delivered 8,928.00
  • share base = fee parts 360.00 − partner cost 72.00 = 288.00; Raeen 50 % = 144.00; NPL remainder 144.00; the 0.3 % rebate (30.00 USDT) is outside the base

12 · Variances, by cause (D29, D45, D46)

expected_out = converted_in × partner_rate_version.rate (as priced) actual_out = what the partner booked (partner_calculates) or expected_out (npl_calculates) variance = expected_out − actual_out (+ = NPL received less than priced) client_net unchanged (write-once); pool = actual_out − client_net; the variance posts to VAR_CONVERSION partner_calculates differences and basis variance on a reroute VAR_CUTOFF rate stamped at lock vs rate used after the partner's cutoff (conversion_due_date) VAR_RATE_HONOUR an expired quote's rate honoured by override vs the day's rate VAR_FX_TIMING an obligation priced on one day and discharged on another (§14)
  • partner_calculates: 3,000 USDT expected at 0.8910 = 2,673.00, booked at 0.8905 = 2,671.50; variance = 1.50 → VAR_CONVERSION; client net 2,646.00 unchanged
  • cutoff timing: locked 0.8910, converted next day at 0.8890 → 6.00 → VAR_CUTOFF
  • honoured expired rate: 0.8910 honoured, Ali at 0.8860 → 15.00 → VAR_RATE_HONOUR

13 · Earnings retained in the collection currency and offset between partners (D46)

retained_in = earnings ÷ partner_rate (this deal's margin kept unconverted, residual_in) offset_to = from_amount ÷ to_partner_rate_of_day (EARNINGS_OFFSET: NPL's margin at the from-partner, in USDT at the to-partner) converted_in = amount_in − retained_in − offset_to client_out = converted_in × partner_rate = client_net − from_amount already delivered at the from-partner neutral for the client: Σ client entitlement unchanged; NPL's EARN_GROSS for the deal is recognised in the collection currency
  • NPL's margin at Jeton (EUR) 2,025.00 ÷ Ali's rate of the day 0.8910 = 2,272.73 USDT taken from the client's unconverted balance at Ali
  • Ali deal margin 900.00 EUR ÷ 0.8910 = 1,010.10 USDT retained (residual_in)
  • Ali converts 100,000 − 2,272.73 − 1,010.10 = 96,717.17 USDT × 0.8910 = 86,175.00 EUR = client net 88,200.00 − 2,025.00 already delivered at Jeton

14 · Invoices paid in instalments and the forex true-up (D24, D46)

reserved_i = obligation_discharged_i × DEAL_GROUP.invoice_rate (payout currency per invoice currency at pricing: the stored base) paid_i = obligation_discharged_i × SETTLEMENT_LINE.discharge_rate_i (same direction, at payment) true_up_i = reserved_i − paid_i (+ gain, − loss) → VAR_FX_TIMING against NPL's pool INVOICE_BALANCE = vendor invoice: amount − Σ obligation_discharged over released lines not returned; resale invoice: amount − Σ DEAL_GROUP.invoice_amount collected
  • invoice_rate = 1.148369 USDT per EUR (0.8708 the other way); instalment 1: 3,000 EUR × 1.148369 = 3,445.11 USDT paid at the same rate it was reserved at; true-up 0
  • instalment 2: discharge_rate 1.142505; paid 6,000 × 1.142505 = 6,855.03; reserved 6,000 × 1.148369 = 6,890.22; true-up = 35.19 USDT gain → VAR_FX_TIMING (a debit when the rate moves against NPL)
  • margin line: 450 EUR × 1.142505 = 514.13; reserved 450 × 1.148369 = 516.77; true-up 2.64

15 · The reseller project (NPL-GR)

reseller_margin = resale_invoice − its vendor_invoice (INVOICE.vendor_invoice_id) (EUR; fixed when the two invoices are posted) target_out = resale_invoice ÷ pricing_rate (USDT to deliver) amount_in = target_out × (1 + remittance_markup) ÷ market_rate (§6; cash-rounded if THB cash) remittance_margin = earnings (§8) + Σ true_up_i (§14) − fees (NPL's example: 2 % − 1 % Ali − 0.1 % forex = 0.9 %…1.2 %) NPL_PROFIT = Σ remittance_margin + Σ reseller_margin (per currency; one figure through REPORTING_VALUE)

16 · Pools, squaring, loss splits, balance conversion, returns, network fees

squaring : pool_ccy < 0 after an absorbed fee → next conversion includes (−pool_ccy ÷ partner_rate) of NPL's own USDT loss split : sender_share + npl_share + partner_share + client_share = LOSS_EVENT.amount ≤ amount verified or released balance conv. : out = amount × partner_rate (no fee unless a FEE_DECISION applies §3); rounding as the pair says return leg : out = returned_amount × partner_rate; markup only by FEE_DECISION; the first conversion's margin stays earned bank fee : treatment waived (client bears) | carry_forward (SHORTFALL, topped up) | absorbed or covered (NPL, out of the pool) network fee : USDT; zero and unposted when none applies; absorbed by NPL or charged back to the sender per project policy
  • bounce fee 15.00 GBP absorbed with no GBP pool at Ali → pool −15.00; squared at the next conversion: 15.00 ÷ 0.7722 = 19.43 USDT of NPL's own converted alongside the client's
  • loss 10,000.00 USDT → EXP_LOSS; split partner 4,000.00 + sender 2,000.00 + client 1,000.00 + NPL 3,000.00 = 10,000.00; EXP_LOSS after recoveries = 3,000.00

17 · Reporting values (D43)

REPORTING_VALUE(figure, ccy, as_of) = figure × latest market_rate(platform pair ccy → reporting_currency), with the rate, its time and a stale flag never posted, quoted or settled; the only control reading it is a ceiling stated in the reporting currency

20 · Acceptance

  1. The engine reproduces every vector of §18 and §19 exactly (to the cent, or to the unit where the sheet rounds), and every posting amount of the Ledger Posting Design v5.7.
  2. Fee parts are computed on the principal (§3) and never compounded; a target-out quote applies the fee as a markup on the target (§6).
  3. Every rounding step uses the pair's configured option and records the signed residual with its owner (§9).
  4. Variances are posted to the account of their cause (§12); the forex true-up of each instalment is posted per line (§14); an earnings offset leaves every client entitlement unchanged (§13).
  5. NPL_PROFIT returns remittance earnings, reseller earnings and their sum, per currency, and the same figures through REPORTING_VALUE (§15, §17).
