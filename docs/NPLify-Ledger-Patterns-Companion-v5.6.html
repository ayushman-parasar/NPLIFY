How to read this document

Every account in the ledger is a jar with a label. Some jars hold money (at a partner, in NPL's wallet, in transit). Some hold promises (what NPL has undertaken to deliver, what someone owes NPL). Two hold NPL's results: what it has earned, and what it has cost. Every event moves value out of one jar and into another, and the amounts always match, so nothing ever appears from nowhere or disappears.

Each example is one event, shown as a table. The columns:

  • Account — the jar, written as owner · place · currency · purpose. CL jars hold the client's money; CO jars are NPL's.
  • Debit / Credit — the bookkeeper's two columns. You can ignore them.
  • In / Out — the same thing in a word: IN means value goes to this jar, OUT means it comes from this jar. The word after the dot says what kind of jar it is.
  • Owner — whose value it is, the client's or NPL's. Component — what kind of value: principal (the money itself), earnings, a cost, a fee, and so on.
  • Why — the reason for the line, in a few words.

Read the explanation on the right first, then look for each sentence in the table. A tall table has its explanation above it instead.

The jars that appear most
COLLECTEDclient money that has arrived at a partner and is not yet converted
DUEclient money, converted, waiting at a partner to be paid out
INTRANSITclient money sent out and not yet confirmed as received
HELDclient money sitting in one of NPL's own wallets (allowed in three cases only)
PAYABLEthe promise: what NPL still has to deliver for the client, per currency
CREDIT / SHORTFALLsmaller promises: owed back to a sender; still owed to a receiver after a short delivery
POOLNPL's own money resting at a partner, mostly its fees, invisible to the partner
WALLETNPL's own money in its own wallets
EARN_GROSS / EARN_REBATEwhat NPL has earned: its fees; rebates from partners, kept separate
EXP_…what NPL has borne: partner fees, bank fees, network fees, rounding, losses
VAR_…differences between what was expected and what happened, by cause
…_RECEIVABLE / …_PAYABLEwhat others owe NPL, and what NPL owes others, for shares, rebates, losses, invoices

Pattern A — Direct collection, conversion and accumulation

The everyday deal. A sender pays a partner, the partner converts the money, and the converted money waits at the partner until it is paid out. The fee is taken at the moment of conversion, and that is the only moment when some of the client's money becomes NPL's.
T1 · Collection of deal 1: 10,000 USDT arrive at Ali's USDT wallet endpoint (COLLECTION_RECEIVING_ENDPOINT, kind wallet) source: Collection
AccountDebitCreditIn / OutCcyOwnerComponentWhy
CL.ALI.USDT.COLLECTED10,000.00IN · moneyUSDTclientprincipalclient money now sits at Ali, unconverted; the account is per partner, the endpoint only says which vehicle's wallet
CL.PROJECT.USDT.PAYABLE10,000.00OUT · promiseUSDTclientprincipalNPL owes this onward on the client's behalf
What is happening
A sender has just paid 10,000 USDT into Ali's wallet. Two things are now true at once: the money is sitting at Ali (money IN to the "Ali, USDT" jar) and NPL has promised to deliver it onward for the client (a promise OUT of the "we owe the receivers" jar). Nothing is earned yet. The money is the client's; NPL is only holding it.
T2 · Conversion of deal 1 at Ali (fee_in_rate): 10,000 USDT → 8,910.00 EUR at 0.891 source: Conversion
AccountDebitCreditIn / OutCcyOwnerComponentWhy
CL.PROJECT.USDT.PAYABLE10,000.00IN · promiseUSDTclientprincipalthe USDT obligation is discharged by the conversion
CL.ALI.USDT.COLLECTED10,000.00OUT · moneyUSDTclientprincipalthe USDT left the collected position
CL.ALI.EUR.DUE8,820.00IN · moneyEURclientprincipalclient net in whole euros, waiting to be settled
CO.ALI.EUR.POOL90.00IN · moneyEURcompanyearningsNPL's margin, physically still at Ali (EARNINGS_RECEIVABLE)
CL.PROJECT.EUR.PAYABLE8,820.00OUT · promiseEURclientprincipalowed to the receiver group, now in EUR
CO.NONE.EUR.EARN_GROSS90.00OUT · earnedEURcompanyearningsmargin recognised: the client's claim shrinks from 10,000 USDT to its EUR entitlement, the difference is NPL's
What is happening
Ali turns the USDT into euros. The USDT promise is torn up (IN · promise) and the USDT leaves Ali's USDT jar. What arrives in euros is split two ways: 8,820.00 into the client's EUR jar at Ali, and 90.00 EUR into NPL's own jar at Ali (its pool). A new promise is made in euros, 8,820.00, which is what the client was quoted. The 90.00 is NPL's fee: this is the one moment client money becomes NPL's. Ali's own cost is hidden inside its rate, so it never appears as a line.
T3a · Same-currency collection at Ali: 5,000 EUR by bank transfer (as T1) source: Collection
AccountDebitCreditIn / OutCcyOwnerComponentWhy
CL.ALI.EUR.COLLECTED5,000.00IN · moneyEURclientprincipal
CL.PROJECT.EUR.PAYABLE5,000.00OUT · promiseEURclientprincipal
What is happening
A plain bank transfer of 5,000 EUR arrives at Ali for a deal where the sender pays in euros and the receiver gets euros. Same shape as T1: money IN at Ali, a promise OUT.
T3 · Same-currency pass-through (D33): the 5,000 EUR converted at rate 1 — fee 2.00 % captured, client net 4,900.00 EUR (the collection posted as in T1 and is not repeated) source: Conversion
AccountDebitCreditIn / OutCcyOwnerComponentWhy
CL.PROJECT.EUR.PAYABLE5,000.00IN · promiseEURclientprincipalthe obligation is re-stated at the sender's price
CL.ALI.EUR.COLLECTED5,000.00OUT · moneyEURclientprincipal
CL.ALI.EUR.DUE4,900.00IN · moneyEURclientprincipalclient net, available to settle
CO.ALI.EUR.POOL100.00IN · moneyEURcompanyearnings
CL.PROJECT.EUR.PAYABLE4,900.00OUT · promiseEURclientprincipal
CO.NONE.EUR.EARN_GROSS100.00OUT · earnedEURcompanyearningsthe fee is earned at conversion even though no currency changes
What is happening
Even though no currency changes, the deal still "converts" at a rate of 1. That is how the fee is taken: of the 5,000, 4,900.00 is the client's (promise and jar in euros) and 100.00 is NPL's fee into its pool. Without this step there would be no moment at which the fee is earned.

Pattern B — Part collection

A sender may pay a deal in several instalments. Each instalment is recorded as it arrives; the conversion waits until the deal is fully paid.
T4 · Collection of deal 2, part 1 of 2: 4,000 USDT source: Collection
AccountDebitCreditIn / OutCcyOwnerComponentWhy
CL.ALI.USDT.COLLECTED4,000.00IN · moneyUSDTclientprincipalfirst part
CL.PROJECT.USDT.PAYABLE4,000.00OUT · promiseUSDTclientprincipal
What is happening
The sender pays a deal of 5,000 USDT in two instalments. The first 4,000 arrive: money IN at Ali, promise OUT for the same amount. The deal is not complete, so nothing converts yet.
T5 · Collection of deal 2, part 2 of 2: 1,000 USDT (next day) source: Collection
AccountDebitCreditIn / OutCcyOwnerComponentWhy
CL.ALI.USDT.COLLECTED1,000.00IN · moneyUSDTclientprincipalsecond part completes the deal
CL.PROJECT.USDT.PAYABLE1,000.00OUT · promiseUSDTclientprincipal
What is happening
The remaining 1,000 arrive the next day. Same two lines. Now the whole 5,000 is at Ali and the promise is 5,000.
T6 · Conversion of deal 2 at Ali: 5,000 USDT → 4,455.00 EUR source: Conversion
AccountDebitCreditIn / OutCcyOwnerComponentWhy
CL.PROJECT.USDT.PAYABLE5,000.00IN · promiseUSDTclientprincipal
CL.ALI.USDT.COLLECTED5,000.00OUT · moneyUSDTclientprincipal
CL.ALI.EUR.DUE4,410.00IN · moneyEURclientprincipal
CO.ALI.EUR.POOL45.00IN · moneyEURcompanyearnings
CL.PROJECT.EUR.PAYABLE4,410.00OUT · promiseEURclientprincipal
CO.NONE.EUR.EARN_GROSS45.00OUT · earnedEURcompanyearnings
What is happening
With the deal fully paid, Ali converts the whole 5,000 at once, exactly like T2: USDT out, euros in for the client, NPL's fee into its pool, the promise re-stated in euros. A cash deal would wait here until the partner has counted the notes.

Pattern C — Settlement, confirmation, bank fees, shortfalls, rounding

Paying out: the partner sends the money, the receiver confirms it arrived, and any bank fee or rounding difference is settled according to the rules of the project.
T7 · Settlement from Ali: 18,130.00 EUR in two lines (Entity X1 8,000.00, Entity X2 10,130.00) source: Settlement
AccountDebitCreditIn / OutCcyOwnerComponentWhy
CL.ALI.EUR.INTRANSIT8,000.00IN · moneyEURclientprincipalline 1 released by Ali
CL.ALI.EUR.INTRANSIT10,130.00IN · moneyEURclientprincipalline 2 released by Ali
CL.ALI.EUR.DUE18,130.00OUT · moneyEURclientprincipalwhole euros leave DUE; 0.00 EUR of rounding dust stays
What is happening
Ali now pays out. The client's euro jar at Ali holds several converted deals; NPL sends 18,130.00 in two bank transfers, 8,000.00 to one of the receiver's accounts and 10,130.00 to another. The money moves from "at Ali" to "in transit" (sent, not yet confirmed). Only whole euros go out; 0.00 of cents stays behind, still the client's. The promise is untouched: nothing is delivered until the receiver confirms.
T8 · Confirmation, line 1 full: Entity X1 received 8,000.00 EUR source: Confirmation
AccountDebitCreditIn / OutCcyOwnerComponentWhy
CL.PROJECT.EUR.PAYABLE8,000.00IN · promiseEURclientprincipalthe obligation is discharged
CL.ALI.EUR.INTRANSIT8,000.00OUT · moneyEURclientprincipalthe money reached the receiver
What is happening
The receiver confirms the first transfer arrived in full. The money leaves "in transit" and the promise is reduced by the same 8,000.00: delivered means NPL no longer owes it.
T9 · Confirmation, line 2 short: Entity X2 received 10,080.00 EUR, the bank took 50.00 (treatment carry_forward) source: Confirmation
AccountDebitCreditIn / OutCcyOwnerComponentWhy
CL.PROJECT.EUR.PAYABLE10,080.00IN · promiseEURclientprincipaldelivered part
CL.RECEIVER_X.EUR.SHORTFALL50.00IN · moneyEURclientbank_feestill owed to the receiver, topped up at the next settlement
CL.ALI.EUR.INTRANSIT10,130.00OUT · moneyEURclientprincipalnothing is left in transit
What is happening
The second transfer arrives 50.00 short because a bank took a fee on the way. 10,080.00 counts as delivered (promise reduced). The missing 50.00 is parked in a "shortfall" jar for that receiver: NPL still owes it and will top it up next time. If the client had agreed to bear bank fees, the promise would simply drop by the full amount instead.
T10 · Shortfall top-up funded from NPL's pool at Ali (BANK_FEE_EVENT applied to the next settlement) source: BankFeeEvent
AccountDebitCreditIn / OutCcyOwnerComponentWhy
CO.NONE.EUR.EXP_BANKFEE50.00IN · costEURcompanybank_feeNPL bears the cost
CO.ALI.EUR.POOL50.00OUT · moneyEURcompanybank_feepaid out of NPL's money resting at Ali
CL.ALI.EUR.DUE50.00IN · moneyEURclientbank_feethe 50 EUR is client money again, in balance at Ali
CL.RECEIVER_X.EUR.SHORTFALL50.00OUT · moneyEURclientbank_feethe shortfall is cleared
What is happening
NPL makes the shortfall good from its own money at Ali. 50.00 goes IN to NPL's cost jar (a bank fee NPL bears) and OUT of NPL's pool at Ali; the same 50.00 goes back IN to the client's jar at Ali and the shortfall jar is emptied. This is the rare direction: NPL's money becoming client money. It goes out with the next payout like any other balance.
T11 · Conversion with round-up (D25): 3,000 USDT → 96,810.00 THB at 32.27 (market 32.6); client net 95,844.00, paid out as 96,000.00 (round_up_n, unit 1,000) source: Conversion
AccountDebitCreditIn / OutCcyOwnerComponentWhy
CL.PROJECT.USDT.PAYABLE3,000.00IN · promiseUSDTclientprincipal(collection of the 3,000 USDT assumed posted as T1)
CL.ALI.USDT.COLLECTED3,000.00OUT · moneyUSDTclientprincipal
CL.ALI.THB.DUE95,844.00IN · moneyTHBclientprincipalclient net as priced
CL.ALI.THB.DUE156.00IN · moneyTHBclientroundingthe amount added by rounding up: client money from now on
CO.ALI.THB.POOL810.00IN · moneyTHBcompanyearningsNPL's margin less the round-up it funded
CO.NONE.THB.EXP_ROUNDING156.00IN · costTHBcompanyroundingthe cost of rounding up (rounding_residual is negative)
CL.PROJECT.THB.PAYABLE96,000.00OUT · promiseTHBclientprincipalthe obligation is the rounded payout
CO.NONE.THB.EARN_GROSS966.00OUT · earnedTHBcompanyearningsmargin as priced
What is happening
A Thai-baht payout that must be rounded up to the nearest 1,000 baht. The client is owed 95,844.00 but will be paid 96,000.00. The extra 156.00 is NPL's cost (rounding jar) and comes out of NPL's margin, so NPL's pool gets 810.00 instead of 966.00. Rounding down (T2) is the opposite case: the leftover cents stay the client's.

Pattern D — Contingency routes and the bridge wallet

Sometimes money has to pass through one of NPL's own wallets: when the usual partner is unavailable, or when the sender can only pay on a network the partner does not accept. The ledger tracks it there until it leaves.
T12 · Deal 3, Ali unavailable: 10,000 USDT collected into NPL's contingency wallet under an approved REROUTE source: Collection
AccountDebitCreditIn / OutCcyOwnerComponentWhy
CL.OWN.USDT.HELD10,000.00IN · moneyUSDTclientprincipalclient money in custody, with deal, reason and age
CL.PROJECT.USDT.PAYABLE10,000.00OUT · promiseUSDTclientprincipal
What is happening
Ali is unavailable, so with management's approval the sender pays 10,000 USDT into NPL's own contingency wallet instead. The money goes IN to a "held in NPL's wallet" jar, and the usual promise goes OUT. Money in NPL's own wallet is allowed only in three named cases; this is one of them, and a custody report watches it until it leaves.
T13 · Forward to Jeton with recover dues: 10,000 USDT of client money credited at Jeton, 30.00 USDT of NPL's dues netted source: Reroute
AccountDebitCreditIn / OutCcyOwnerComponentWhy
CL.JETON.USDT.COLLECTED10,000.00IN · moneyUSDTclientprincipalJeton credits the full client amount
CL.OWN.USDT.HELD10,000.00OUT · moneyUSDTclientprincipalcustody ends
CO.OWN.USDT.WALLET30.00IN · moneyUSDTcompanyearningsNPL keeps 30 USDT in its wallet instead of sending it
CO.JETON.USDT.POOL30.00OUT · moneyUSDTcompanyearningsJeton's debt to NPL falls by the same 30 USDT
What is happening
NPL forwards the client's money to Jeton. The held jar empties and the "at Jeton, USDT" jar fills with the full 10,000. But NPL physically sends only 9,970.00: Jeton already owed NPL 30.00 of earlier fees, so they net it off. For NPL that is 30.00 IN to its own wallet (kept instead of sent) and 30.00 OUT of what Jeton owed it. Nothing is taken from the client's amount.
T14 · Conversion of deal 3 at Jeton (fee_on_market, 1.25 %): 10,000 USDT → 9,000.00 EUR at market, Jeton keeps 112.50 source: Conversion
AccountDebitCreditIn / OutCcyOwnerComponentWhy
CL.PROJECT.USDT.PAYABLE10,000.00IN · promiseUSDTclientprincipal
CL.JETON.USDT.COLLECTED10,000.00OUT · moneyUSDTclientprincipal
CL.JETON.EUR.DUE8,820.00IN · moneyEURclientprincipalclient net, whole euros
CO.JETON.EUR.POOL67.50IN · moneyEURcompanyearningsNPL's net margin resting at Jeton
CO.NONE.EUR.EXP_PARTNER112.50IN · costEURcompanypartner_costJeton's stated fee, deducted at transaction level
CL.PROJECT.EUR.PAYABLE8,820.00OUT · promiseEURclientprincipal
CO.NONE.EUR.EARN_GROSS180.00OUT · earnedEURcompanyearningsgross margin before partner cost
What is happening
Jeton converts at the market rate and then charges its own fee separately (1.25 %). So the lines show three things: the client's euros (8,820.00) into the client's jar at Jeton, Jeton's fee (112.50) into NPL's cost jar, and NPL's net margin (67.50) into its pool at Jeton. The fee NPL charged in total was 180.00 (the "earned" line); Jeton's cost comes out of it. Compare T2, where Ali's cost is invisible because it is inside Ali's rate.
T15 · Deal 4 (D40): the sender can only pay on ERC-20, which Ali does not accept; 10,000 USDT arrive on NPL's bridge wallet source: Collection
AccountDebitCreditIn / OutCcyOwnerComponentWhy
CL.OWN.USDT.HELD10,000.00IN · moneyUSDTclientprincipalclient money on the bridge (OWN_WALLET.role collection_bridge), to be forwarded within custody_max_hours
CL.PROJECT.USDT.PAYABLE10,000.00OUT · promiseUSDTclientprincipal
What is happening
This sender can only pay on a network Ali does not accept. So NPL lets the money land on its own "bridge" wallet first: money IN to the held jar, promise OUT. It must be forwarded within a set number of hours.
T16 · Hop: the bridge forwards 10,000 USDT to Ali on TRC-20 (a SETTLEMENT_LINE to the partner_transit receiver); gas 0.0012 ETH paid by NPL source: Settlement
AccountDebitCreditIn / OutCcyOwnerComponentWhy
CL.OWN.USDT.INTRANSIT10,000.00IN · moneyUSDTclientprincipalleft the bridge, not yet credited by Ali
CL.OWN.USDT.HELD10,000.00OUT · moneyUSDTclientprincipalcustody ends when Ali credits (T17)
CO.NONE.ETH.EXP_NETWORK0.0012IN · costETHcompanynetwork_feenetwork fee of the hop, NPL's cost (project network_fee_policy)
CO.OWN.ETH.WALLET0.0012OUT · moneyETHcompanynetwork_feepaid from NPL's gas reserve
What is happening
The bridge forwards the 10,000 to Ali on a network Ali does accept. The held jar empties into "in transit from NPL's wallet". Sending costs a tiny network fee in ETH, which NPL pays from its own gas reserve: IN to the cost jar, OUT of NPL's wallet. The client pays nothing for the hop.
T17 · Ali credits the hop: 10,000 USDT now sit at Ali as collected client money source: Confirmation
AccountDebitCreditIn / OutCcyOwnerComponentWhy
CL.ALI.USDT.COLLECTED10,000.00IN · moneyUSDTclientprincipalthe leg continues at its partner exactly as T1
CL.OWN.USDT.INTRANSIT10,000.00OUT · moneyUSDTclientprincipal
What is happening
Ali confirms the money has arrived. "In transit" empties and the client's USDT jar at Ali fills, which is exactly where T1 would have put it. From here the deal continues normally.

Pattern E — Cancellations, reversals and voids

Mistakes are never erased; they are cancelled by an equal and opposite posting. A deal that is cancelled after the money arrived is refunded, with the network fee charged where the project's rules say.
T18 · Erroneous posting: a collection of 2,000 USDT was booked against the wrong deal (as posted) source: Collection
AccountDebitCreditIn / OutCcyOwnerComponentWhy
CL.ALI.USDT.COLLECTED2,000.00IN · moneyUSDTclientprincipalbooked in error
CL.PROJECT.USDT.PAYABLE2,000.00OUT · promiseUSDTclientprincipal
What is happening
A mistake: someone records a 2,000 USDT collection against the wrong deal. The posting looks normal (money IN at Ali, promise OUT), but it is wrong.
T19 · Reversal of T18 (LEDGER_TRANSACTION.reversal_of_id = T18), then T18 is re-posted correctly source: Adjustment
AccountDebitCreditIn / OutCcyOwnerComponentWhy
CL.PROJECT.USDT.PAYABLE2,000.00IN · promiseUSDTclientprincipalmirror image of T18
CL.ALI.USDT.COLLECTED2,000.00OUT · moneyUSDTclientprincipal
What is happening
Nothing is ever deleted. Instead a mirror-image transaction is posted that points at the wrong one: promise IN, money OUT, same amounts. The two cancel. Then the correct posting is made afresh. Anyone looking at history sees the mistake, the reversal and the fix, so the books add up at every past moment.
T20 · Collection of deal 5: 3,000 USDT arrive at Ali (the deal is voided afterwards) source: Collection
AccountDebitCreditIn / OutCcyOwnerComponentWhy
CL.ALI.USDT.COLLECTED3,000.00IN · moneyUSDTclientprincipal
CL.PROJECT.USDT.PAYABLE3,000.00OUT · promiseUSDTclientprincipal
What is happening
A sender pays 3,000 USDT for a deal that is about to be cancelled. Normal collection lines.
T21 · Deal 5 voided after collection: refund to the sender in USDT, network fee 2.00 USDT charged back to the sender (project network_fee_policy) source: Adjustment
AccountDebitCreditIn / OutCcyOwnerComponentWhy
CL.PROJECT.USDT.PAYABLE3,000.00IN · promiseUSDTclientprincipalthe obligation ends
CL.ALI.USDT.COLLECTED2,998.00OUT · moneyUSDTclientprincipalreturned to the sender's wallet
CL.ALI.USDT.COLLECTED2.00OUT · moneyUSDTclientnetwork_feepaid to the network; the sender bears it
What is happening
The deal is voided, so the money must go back. The promise is torn up (IN · promise, the obligation ends) and the money leaves Ali's jar: 2,998.00 back to the sender and 2.00 to the network as the sending fee, which this project charges to the sender. If NPL absorbed the fee instead, the sender would get the full 3,000 and the 2.00 would come out of NPL's pool into its cost jar.

Pattern F — Rejected payout and return leg

A bank can reject a payout. The money comes back, the failed payout is undone, and the client can either try again later or have the money converted back and returned.

CL.PROJECT.USDT.PAYABLE2,000.00OUT · promiseUSDTclientprincipal
What is happening
A deal of 2,000 USDT that will be paid out in pounds. Money IN at Ali, promise OUT.

T24 · The receiving bank rejects the transfer: 1,513.00 GBP come back to Ali, the bank kept 15.00 (SETTLEMENT_RETURN; line outcome = returned) source: SettlementReturn
AccountDebitCreditIn / OutCcyOwnerComponentWhy
CL.ALI.GBP.DUE1,513.00IN · moneyGBPclientprincipalback in the client balance at Ali
CO.NONE.GBP.EXP_BANKFEE15.00IN · costGBPcompanybank_feebounce fee absorbed by NPL (treatment absorbed)

T25 · Return leg: 1,528.00 GBP converted back to 1,963.48 USDT at Ali (rate 1.285); markup waived by FEE_DECISION source: Conversion

What is happening
The client decides to convert the pounds back to USDT and have them returned. The pound promise is torn up and the pounds leave Ali; 1,963.48 USDT arrive in the client's jar with a new USDT promise. NPL waived its fee on this return, so there is no earned line. Note NPL's margin from T22 is untouched: it was earned at the first conversion and a return never claws it back.

Pattern H — Balance conversion

Money already converted and waiting at a partner can be converted again into another currency, without a new sender or receiver, when a payout needs it.
T26 · Opening position: 5,000 SGD of converted client balance at Ali from earlier deals (summarised) source: Conversion
AccountDebitCreditIn / OutCcyOwnerComponentWhy
CL.ALI.SGD.DUE5,000.00IN · moneySGDclientprincipalbalance built up by earlier SGD deals
CL.PROJECT.SGD.PAYABLE5,000.00OUT · promiseSGDclientprincipal
What is happening
Starting point for the next example: the client has 5,000 Singapore dollars sitting at Ali from earlier deals (money in the SGD jar, promise in SGD).
T27 · Balance conversion at Ali: 5,000 SGD of client balance → 3,700.00 USDT at 0.74, no markup (serves Group B) source: BalanceConversion
AccountDebitCreditIn / OutCcyOwnerComponentWhy
CL.PROJECT.SGD.PAYABLE5,000.00IN · promiseSGDclientprincipalSGD obligation ends
CL.ALI.SGD.DUE5,000.00OUT · moneySGDclientprincipalSGD balance leaves
CL.ALI.USDT.DUE3,700.00IN · moneyUSDTclientprincipalUSDT balance arrives, whole units
CL.PROJECT.USDT.PAYABLE3,700.00OUT · promiseUSDTclientprincipalsame obligation, new currency
What is happening
NPL has the balance itself converted to USDT, without any sender or receiver involved, because a receiver needs USDT. The SGD promise is torn up and the SGD leave; 3,700.00 USDT arrive in the client's jar with a USDT promise. No fee here (that would be a separate decision). Finance asks for this, Management approves it.

Pattern I — Overpayment and sender credit

A sender who pays too much gets a credit, which is refunded or used on the next deal.
T28 · Overpayment: a sender sends 10,050 USDT against a 10,000 USDT deal; 50 USDT becomes sender credit source: Collection
AccountDebitCreditIn / OutCcyOwnerComponentWhy
CL.ALI.USDT.COLLECTED10,050.00IN · moneyUSDTclientprincipalall of it is client money at Ali
