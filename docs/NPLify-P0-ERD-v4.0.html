NPLify · P0 Technical Baseline

Deliverable 1 — Entity-Relationship Diagram & Data Model · Draft v4.0

New XP Technologies Limited

7 October 2026 · Confidential

Status: Rebuilt from ERD v3.0 (2 October 2026), the NPLify — Project Understanding v1.0 with Sud's review comments, and the ERD review of 6–7 October 2026 that traced three operational scenarios (a payout rejected by the receiving bank, a two-leg route across two partners, an ad-hoc conversion of a client balance) against the model. Supersedes v3.0 in full.
Convention: all monetary amounts stored as exact decimals; every table carries created_at / created_by; audit, approvals and locking are modelled once (domain 4) and apply to all master data. Derived figures (client balance, group entitlement, offsets, custody) are views over postings and deals — never stored.

Six diagrams across four domains, one schema: 55 tables and 4 views. Grey dashed boxes in a diagram mark entities defined in another diagram, and name it. Arrows run in the direction of data flow, from the referenced row into the row that stores the foreign key; a filled arrow is composition (the parent owns the child), an open arrow a reference, a dashed line a polymorphic link resolved by a type column plus an id. Section 6 lists what changed since v3.0; Appendix A reconciles the model with the FRS; Appendix B is the data dictionary.

1 · Configuration domain

A Project (= client) owns its configuration. Partners are global counterparties; how a partner is used in this project is the partner configuration block — fees, vehicles, endpoints, registrations and rebate terms hang off it. Receivers are organised in receiver groups, the unit entitlement is tracked on, and each receiving entity holds one or more accounts with a currency and rail.

Notes

  • A Project (= client) owns its configuration. Receivers sit in receiver groups: every project has one default group and every receiver belongs to exactly one group. Entitlement is tracked per group, so payouts within a group are ordinary settlement and only payouts across groups are offsets; a project with a single group never has an offset.
  • A Receiver has a kind: counterparty for a client receiver; sender_return stands for a sender so refunds use the ordinary payout path and the partner's destination approval; partner_transit stands for a partner's collection endpoint so the hop of a two-leg route is an ordinary payout. sender_return receivers live in the project's return group and never appear on forward deals or allow lists.
  • A receiving entity holds one or more accounts, each with a currency and rail (EUR via SWIFT and EUR via SEPA are two accounts). Registrations and payout lines point at the account.
  • Fees are per partner × pair (FEE_STRUCTURE under PARTNER_PAIR), so choosing the partner is part of pricing. PARTNER.disclosure_style records whether the partner's markup is visible; partner_markup_pct is known only for market_plus partners.
  • Three collection-endpoint kinds by method: crypto-in → WALLET; bank-in → COLLECTION_RECEIVING_ENTITY; cash-in → no record (instructions on the deal). Cash collections round to the nearest 100 / 500 / 1000 units by currency (CURRENCY_PAIR.cash_rounding_unit).
  • Destination approval is per partner: SETTLEMENT_REGISTRATION is an account of the project's receiving entity as registered with this partner. SETTLEMENT_SENDING_ENTITY is the narrative on an outgoing transfer; SETTLEMENT_RAIL is the bank/EMI that executes it; neither holds money. COLLECTION_SENDING_ENTITY is provenance only and never a payout destination.
  • OWN_WALLET exists for rerouting and pass-through (AQN-LT-Sub is an own wallet at Aquanow); PARTNER_CONFIG.applicable_for_reroute defines the alternate-partner set. INTRODUCER / REFERRAL_RULE record referral commissions NPL pays; PARTNER_REBATE_RULE records rebates a partner pays NPL.

2 · Rates domain

Notes

  • The rate comparison for the day is created lazily by the first deal that needs it, one per partner × pair × date, and reused by every deal that day. A partner may give a new rate during the day, so one date can hold several versions. Rolling averages are built from confirmed versions only.
  • A version is breached when spread > avg_2w × (1 + variance_threshold_rel); quoting and conversion on that partner × pair freeze until Finance approves with a reason, rejects, or asks for a better rate. Versions are never overwritten; every deal leg, conversion and balance conversion references its exact version.
  • Cross-source slippage is a query over MARKET_RATE across sources for the same pair and window.

3 · Deal lifecycle domain

3a · Deal group, legs, collection, conversion, reroute, balance conversion

3b · Disbursement, return, confirmation, fees, referrals and rebates

Derived views (never stored)

ViewDefinition
CLIENT_BALANCE(project, partner, currency)CLIENT_BALANCE(project, partner, currency) = Σ conversions’ actual_out booked at that partner (deal legs and balance conversions, target side) − Σ balance conversions’ amount_in (source side) + Σ returned amounts − Σ released disbursement totals. Carries rounding dust below one unit.
GROUP_ENTITLEMENT(group, currency)GROUP_ENTITLEMENT(group, currency) = Σ client_net of converted forward legs attributed to the group ± entitlement re-attributions − Σ DISBURSEMENT_LINE.amount paid to receivers in the group + Σ returned amounts. Replaces RECEIVER_ENTITLEMENT. Σ over a project’s groups = Σ CLIENT_BALANCE over partners, per currency.
OFFSET(group, currency)OFFSET(group, currency) = GROUP_ENTITLEMENT; positive = owed, negative = holding another group’s flow. Can only be non-zero in a project with more than one group. Carries Management approval and tighter aging.
CUSTODY(own_wallet)CUSTODY(own_wallet) = Σ collections into the own wallet under open reroutes + Σ recovered dues resting there − Σ forwarded; each row with deal, reason, age. The standing control on contingency holding.

Notes

  • Every deal belongs to a deal group. DEAL_GROUP is the client-facing instruction: the pair as the sender sees it, the sender rate, fee, reference and entry mode. A plain single-partner deal is a group of one leg; a route that needs two partners (INR → USDT at Ali, USDT → EUR at Jeton) has two forward legs; a rejected payout is unwound with a return leg.
  • Legs. leg_type forward or return; funding_source collection, prior_leg (leg 2 of a route, no collection) or returned_disbursement (a return leg). Priced once and partner fixed at quote hold per leg. Attribution is to the receiver group; the receiver is optional until settlement.
  • Two entry modes as before: collect-first legs are born in state Collected with priced_at = conversion time; quote-first legs carry sent / valid / grace timestamps and a late decision. An estimate-first mode gives an indicative figure and prices at conversion.
  • Conversion has exactly one owner: a deal leg or a BALANCE_CONVERSION. The receiver-side fee is taken at conversion. Amounts are truncated to whole units at conversion; the dropped fraction (rounding_residual) stays client money in the balance and pays out when it reaches a whole unit. NPL's margin is captured at conversion and is never again at risk from a return or a forward.
  • Balance conversion is NPL converting a client's held balance (5K SGD → USDT to fund a 15K USDT payout): Finance-initiated, no sender, receiver or collection, same rate machinery, markup by FEE_DECISION. It names the receiver group it serves; ENTITLEMENT_REATTRIBUTION moves that group's entitlement to the new currency, and any excess over the group's entitlement is booked as an approved offset.
  • Reroute is the only way partner or facilitating entity change after quote. Forwarding is forward_full or forward_and_recover_dues: NPL nets what the alternate partner owes it (EARNINGS_RECEIVABLE) against the funds forwarded, within the outstanding receivable, with the partner's agreement recorded. Post-collection outcome is via the own wallet or a refund to the sender.
  • One settlement model: DISBURSEMENT drains one partner's holding into one or many DISBURSEMENT_LINEs, in whole units. Under disburse_policy to_zero the balance after release is less than one unit; under hold_allowed the balance may rest at the partner within the project's exposure cap.
  • A line's paid_receiver_id may be in another group: that is the offset, created by the same action, with Management approval and tighter aging.
  • Rejected payout. When the receiving or intermediary bank rejects a transfer, DISBURSEMENT_RETURN records the returned amount, bank fee and reason; the line's outcome becomes returned, a reversing ledger transaction puts the money back into the client's balance and BANK_FEE_EVENT books the bounce fee. The money then stays in balance for later payouts, or funds a return leg that converts back and pays the sender's return receiver.
  • Fee decisions. FEE_DECISION is a one-off, approved decision on a single subject: markup applied or waived on a return leg or balance conversion, a network-fee override on a refund, a bank-fee treatment override on one settlement. Standing rules stay in FEE_STRUCTURE and FEE_OVERRIDE.
  • CONFIRMATION is full or short; a short confirmation opens a SHORTFALL topped up by a later disbursement. Rounding differences no longer land here. REFERRAL_ACCRUAL and PARTNER_REBATE_ACCRUAL accrue per deal under their rules; rebates are reconciled monthly against the partner's statement.

4 · Ledger & controls domain

Notes

  • Client due balances are held as one account per project × partner × currency (CL.<PARTNER>.<CCY>.DUE); group entitlement is a view, not an account. holder_type never includes a settlement rail or narrative entity.
  • Ownership flips client → company at one event only: margin recognition at conversion. NPL never advances money to a receiver, and withholding on a reroute is not a separate event because the margin is always captured by whichever partner converts.
  • Recover dues on a reroute posts own-wallet cash against the earnings receivable at the partner: company money on both sides, no income.
  • New transaction sources: BalanceConversion, DisbursementReturn, Referral, Rebate. New account purposes: REFERRAL_PAYABLE, REBATE_RECEIVABLE; ADVANCE is removed. New cost components: referral, rounding.
  • APPROVAL, AUDIT_LOG and RECORD_LOCK are modelled once and apply to all master data. Approver ≠ initiator on every approval; Operations never sees derived economics. New approval actions: refund_to_third_party, cross_group_payout, balance_conversion, recover_dues.

5 · Invariants (enforced at write time)

  1. Postings in one transaction sum to zero per currency.
  2. Ledger tables are append-only; corrections are reversing transactions.
  3. No custody in the normal path: CL.OWN.*.HELD may only be debited by a collection on a deal with an approved REROUTE; the custody aging view is the standing control.
  4. Disburse to less than one unit: under disburse_policy = to_zero a DISBURSEMENT pays whole units and leaves only rounding dust; under hold_allowed the balance may rest at the partner within the project’s exposure cap (Q5, Q8).
  5. Priced once, per leg: a leg’s pricing fields are write-once after priced_at; a re-quote is a new leg or a new deal group.
  6. Partner fixed at quote, per leg: partner_pair_id / facilitating_entity_id change only through an approved REROUTE.
  7. Entitlement conservation: Σ GROUP_ENTITLEMENT over a project’s groups = Σ CLIENT_BALANCE over partners, per currency, including balance-conversion re-attributions.
  8. Destination approval precedes collection: a quote-first deal cannot leave Inquiry unless at least one account in the entitled group has an approved SETTLEMENT_REGISTRATION with the chosen partner.
  9. Approver ≠ initiator on every APPROVAL; Operations never receives derived-economics fields.
  10. Every balance shown anywhere is a sum over postings or deals.
  11. A collect-first deal requires an existing SENDER_RECEIVER_ALLOW entry for its sender and group (Q1).
  12. A return leg pays the sender_return RECEIVER linked to the parent leg’s sender, unless a Management-approved exception with a reason names another approved receiver.
  13. The failed forward line is reversed before a return leg opens, so entitlement and balance are correct at the return conversion.
  14. A CONVERSION belongs to exactly one owner: a deal leg or a balance conversion.
  15. A recovery on a reroute cannot exceed the outstanding EARNINGS_RECEIVABLE at that partner; the alternate partner’s expected conversion is computed on the net amount forwarded.
  16. Ownership flips client → company at one event only: margin recognition at conversion. NPL never advances money to a receiver (Q13).
  17. Offsets exist only across receiver groups and carry Management approval and tighter aging; within a group nothing is tracked beyond an informational tally.

6 · What changed from v3.0

12 new tables, 16 changed tables, 3 removals and one view replaced. Field-level detail is in Appendix B.

New tables

TablePurpose
INTRODUCERAn external introducer who brought in a client and is owed referral commission (Understanding §4.2a). Paid either a share of NPL’s earnings or a fixed % per transaction; several introducers may share one deal.
RECEIVER_GROUPThe unit entitlement is tracked on (design F). Every project has one default group created with it; receivers sit in the default group unless further groups are created and receivers are allocated to them. Payouts within a group are ordinary settlement; across groups they are offsets. With a single group no offset can arise. Each project also has a group of kind return that holds its sender_return receivers.
RECEIVING_ENTITY_ACCOUNTOne account of a receiving entity, with its own currency and rail (Q4: EUR via SWIFT and EUR via SEPA are two accounts). Registrations and payout lines point at the account. A sender_return receiver’s USDT wallet is an account like any other.
REFERRAL_RULEHow an introducer is paid on a project (and optionally only on one partner × pair): a share of NPL’s earnings in a ratio, or a fixed % applied inside the sender-facing markup (§4.2a).
PARTNER_REBATE_RULEA partner rebating part of its fee to NPL (§4.2b: Jeton charges 1.25% and gives back 0.4%). Reconciled monthly through PARTNER_REBATE_ACCRUAL.
DEAL_GROUPThe client-facing instruction the legs fulfil: the pair as the sender sees it (INR → EUR), the sender rate, fee and reference. Every deal belongs to a group; a plain single-partner deal is a group of one leg. Margin across a two-leg route is the group quote minus the partner conversions.
BALANCE_CONVERSIONNPL converting a client’s held balance at a partner (5K SGD → USDT to fund a 15K USDT payout). Not a deal leg: no sender, receiver or collection; Finance requests and approves. Uses the same rate machinery; markup is a FEE_DECISION. Names the receiver group it serves (chosen split).
ENTITLEMENT_REATTRIBUTIONWhich group’s entitlement moved currency in a balance conversion, so entitlement per currency still equals balance per currency (invariant 7). The excess over the served group’s source-currency entitlement is another group’s flow and is booked as an approved offset.
DISBURSEMENT_RETURNA payout line the receiving or intermediary bank rejected; the money came back to the partner minus bank fees. Re-enters the balance by a reversing ledger transaction; the fee is booked through BANK_FEE_EVENT. May fund a return leg (funding_source = returned_disbursement) or simply stay in balance for later payouts.
FEE_DECISIONA one-off, approved fee decision on a single subject: markup applied or waived on a return leg or balance conversion, a network-fee override on a refund (Q7), a bank-fee treatment override on one settlement. Standing rules stay in FEE_STRUCTURE and FEE_OVERRIDE.
REFERRAL_ACCRUALReferral commission accrued to an introducer on one deal under a REFERRAL_RULE; paid out of NPL’s earnings.
PARTNER_REBATE_ACCRUALRebate expected from a partner on one deal, reconciled monthly against the partner’s statement and received (usually in crypto). Feeds the Partner Commissions report.
