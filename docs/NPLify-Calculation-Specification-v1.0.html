This document states every calculation the engine performs as a precise formula, in the order it is performed, with the rounding rule that applies at each step, and gives reference vectors computed from those formulas. It is the acceptance yardstick: the engine passes when it reproduces every vector to the cent.
What it does not yet contain: NPL's reference workbooks per project archetype (open question 11) and the reconciliation of the FRS worked figures (open question 9). When they arrive, their cases are added to Section 9 as further vectors and the formulas are confirmed or corrected against them.

1 · Conventions

  • Rates are written as OUT units per 1 IN unit (EUR per USDT for a USDT → EUR deal). A pair configured the other way round (quote_direction = in_per_out) is inverted once, at full precision, before any formula below is applied: R = 1 / R_quoted.
  • Percentages are decimals in formulas (0.40 % is 0.004).
  • Arithmetic is exact decimal arithmetic, never binary floating point. Intermediate results are not rounded. Rounding happens only where this document says so.
  • Symbols: A_in amount collected (IN currency); R_m market reference rate; R_p partner rate; R_b basis rate; s sender share; r receiver share; f = s + r total client-side fee; m partner markup where the partner states market + m.

2 · Fee basis

The fee structure for a partner × pair names a basis: the rate NPL's price is built on.

R_b = R_m if rate_basis = market R_b = R_p if rate_basis = partner

With partner basis NPL's margin is exactly the client-side fee, whatever the partner's spread. With market basis the partner's spread against market eats into the margin, which is why the minimum-margin check (Section 7) exists and why variable fee mode (Section 4) adjusts the fee to the spread.

3 · Sender and receiver split

The total fee f is split into a sender share s, embedded in the rate the sender sees, and a receiver share r, deducted from what the receiver is entitled to. The two are applied one after the other, so the client net is A_in × R_b × (1 − s) × (1 − r), slightly more than A_in × R_b × (1 − f) by the cross term s × r.

sender_rate R_s = R_b × (1 − s) stored to the pair's rate_precision gross_out = round2( A_in × R_s ) what the sender's price promises fee_receiver_part = round2( gross_out × r ) client_net = gross_out − fee_receiver_part the receiver group's entitlement fee_sender_part = round2( A_in × R_b ) − gross_out the sender share, in the OUT currency

round2 rounds half up to the OUT currency's minor unit (2 decimals for fiat; 6 for USDT; 8 for BTC). Where the deal has a per-sender FEE_OVERRIDE, its pct replaces f and is split in the structure's s : r proportion. Where an introducer is paid a fixed percentage inside the sender-facing markup, that percentage is added to s before the sender rate is computed (Section 8).

4 · Fixed and variable fee modes

Fixed: f is the structure's pct. Variable: f is set per deal to protect the margin floor against the partner's current spread, within the structure's floor and cap.

partner_spread σ = 1 − R_p / R_m what the partner keeps relative to market required = min_margin_pct + σ if rate_basis = market = min_margin_pct if rate_basis = partner f = clamp( required, floor_pct, cap_pct ) below_margin = ( f < required ) → the quote proceeds only with Finance approval split s = f × sender_share_pct / pct ; r = f − s (same proportion as the structure)

5 · What the partner delivers, and NPL's earnings

disclosed-rate partner (margin inside its rate): gross_converted = round2( A_in × R_p ) partner_cost = 0 in the books; inferred_cost = round2( A_in × (R_m − R_p) ) kept as information market + m partner (fee stated separately): gross_converted = round2( A_in × R_m ) partner_cost = round2( gross_converted × m ) in both cases: actual_out = gross_converted − partner_cost what the partner books for the client gross_margin = gross_converted − client_net = partner_cost + NPL earnings earnings = actual_out − client_net NPL's margin, recognised at conversion margin_pct = earnings / actual_out

When the partner calculates (calc_mode = partner_calculates), actual_out is whatever the partner booked; expected_out is the formula above; variance = expected_out − actual_out and an exception opens if |variance| / expected_out exceeds the project's rate-variation tolerance. The client net never changes: the sender's price is fixed, so a variance is NPL's cost or gain.

6 · Target-out (quote-first) and collect-first

A quote-first sender asks for an outcome; the quote works backwards to the amount to collect. A collect-first sender simply pays and the formulas of Sections 3 and 5 run forwards at conversion time.

target_out mode: A_in = ceil_in( target_out / ( R_b × (1 − s) × (1 − r) ) ) fixed_in mode: client_net as in Section 3

ceil_in rounds up to the IN currency's minor unit so the sender never under-delivers the target. Example from the FRS: target 1,000 USD, 1 : 1 rate, 0.40 % fee all on the sender side → A_in = 1004.016064 → 1004.02 USDT; the same deal collect-first with 1,000 USDT yields 996.00 USD. Example at PT: to deliver a client net of 8,860.71 EUR, collect 9999.994583 → 9999.994583 USDT.

Quote clocks. Freshness: the quote package must be marked sent within quote_freshness_min of the rate snapshot. Validity: quote_validity_min from sending. Grace: a further grace_min, honoured only if the market has drifted no more than grace_drift_pct: |R_m(now) − R_m(quote)| / R_m(quote) ≤ grace_drift_pct. Beyond grace, the late decision is a re-quote (new deal reference) or, where the project allows, honouring the old rate with Finance approval. The original quote figures never change.

8 · Referral commissions, rebates, markup decisions

fixed-percentage introducer: s_total = s + ref_pct (added to the sender share before R_s) referral_payable = round2( A_in × R_b × ref_pct ) share-of-earnings introducers: referral_payable_i = round2( earnings × share_i ), Σ share_i ≤ 1 partner rebate: rebate = round2( partner_cost × pct_of_partner_fee ) accrued per deal, reconciled monthly return leg / balance conversion markup (FEE_DECISION): converted = round2( A_in × R_p ) earnings = round2( converted × pct ) if decision = apply or override, else 0 to client = converted − earnings

Example: on deal 3 (Section 9, V3) an introducer with a 25 % share of earnings is owed 11.20 EUR; Jeton's 32 % rebate on its 45.00 EUR fee is 14.40 EUR.

9 · Rounding and precision rules

QuantityRule
RatesStored and used at the pair's rate_precision: 5 decimals standard, 8 for sub-unit pairs such as USDT → BTC. Inversion for in_per_out pairs happens at full precision before storing.
Intermediate valuesExact decimals, no rounding.
Money amountsRounded half up to the currency's minor unit when stored: fiat 2 decimals, USDT 6, BTC 8.
Amount to collect (target-out)Rounded up to the IN currency's minor unit.
Converted amounts and payoutsTruncated to whole units at conversion (30.152 → 30). The dropped fraction is rounding_residual, stays client money in the balance, and is paid when accumulated dust reaches a whole unit. Disbursements pay whole units. Sub-unit differences never go to the shortfall ledger.
Cash collectionsRounded to the nearest cash_rounding_unit of the currency: 1000 for INR, 100 for SGD and USD, as configured per currency.
Percentages shownDisplay only; the stored decimal is what the engine uses.
ComparisonsThresholds are compared on exact decimals; "greater than" is strict.

11 · Acceptance

  1. The engine reproduces every reference vector in Section 10 exactly, and every posting amount in the Ledger Posting Design.
  2. For each project archetype, NPL's reference workbook (open question 11) is converted into vectors of the same shape and reproduced exactly; any difference is resolved by correcting either the workbook or this specification, never by tolerance.
  3. The FRS worked figures (open question 9: 1,208 vs 1,225; 258,875 vs 300,000) are reconciled and added as golden cases.
  4. Property checks on random inputs: debits equal credits per currency on every generated transaction; client_net + earnings + partner_cost = gross_converted; whole units + residual = client_net; entitlement conservation after any sequence of conversions, payouts, returns and balance conversions.
