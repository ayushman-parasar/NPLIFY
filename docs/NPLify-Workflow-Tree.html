Level 0Every workflow, as one tree

The root splits into 8 classes of workflow, each class into sub-classes, and each sub-class into the single procedures (92 in all). A procedure names who initiates, who approves, when Management is added on top, what a rejection means, the ROLE_RIGHT code that carries the right, the codes it is written under in the Operating Procedures (C1–C10, A.1–A.9, B.1–B.13), the Scenario Catalogue (F1–F10, W1–W8) and the Ledger Posting Design (Patterns A–N), and the records it touches. The order of the classes is the order of a deal's day: set up, rates, quote, collect, convert, settle, exceptions, close.

Class 5 · 2 sub-classes · 7 procedures

Convert

Operations instructs the conversion once the deal is Collected (on a bank-side pair, not before the due date); Finance approves. The receiver's fee part is taken at conversion; NPL's margin is captured here and never again at risk.

5.1 Conversion on a deal leg5.2 Conversions not on a deal
Class 6 · 3 sub-classes · 16 procedures

Settle (pay out)

Operations prepares the settlement: lines to approved destinations, within the group's entitlement, rounded as the pair says. Finance approves; Management also above the limit, for a third same-day slot, for an offset, or as countersignature when only one Finance user is on shift. Release discharges the promise; confirmation closes the line.

6.1 Prepare, approve and release6.2 Payout methods6.3 Bank fees and returns
Class 7 · 5 sub-classes · 12 procedures

Exceptions, custody & company money

Frozen states need a decision, not time: reroutes, custody in NPL's own wallet, voids, loss events, earnings offsets and dues recovery, ledger adjustments. Money that already moved is never rolled back by a rejection.

Class 8 · 4 sub-classes · 12 procedures

Reconcile, close & report

Finance reconciles every evening and at month end; the ledger's identities are the controls. Earlier history enters through the cutover's parallel run and backfill. Earlier history enters as verified opening entries on the opening date, and deals open on that date are entered in their current state (Cutover Approach §4).
