1 · What the ledger is for, in one paragraph

NPL coordinates other people's money and, in one project, is also its own client: NPL-GR resells goods and pays for the remittance like any customer. The ledger answers, at any moment and for any past moment, three questions: where is the money (at which partner, in transit, in one of NPL's wallets), whose money is it (the client's, a sender's, or NPL's), and what did each movement cost or earn (NPL's margin and shares of it, rebates, partner cost, bank and network fees, rounding, losses, and the four kinds of variance). It does this with ordinary double-entry bookkeeping: every movement is a set of debits and credits that balance, nothing is ever edited or deleted, and every balance anyone sees is a sum over those entries.

2 · Rules that every posting follows

  1. One transaction per event. A collection, a conversion, a balance conversion, a settlement, a settlement return, a confirmation, a bank-fee event, a reroute, a markup-share or rebate accrual or settlement, a loss event or recovery, and a manual adjustment each produce exactly one LEDGER_TRANSACTION that points at its source record (source_type, source_id).
  2. Balanced per currency and per owner. Within one transaction the debits equal the credits in each currency separately, and separately again for client-tagged and company-tagged lines. A conversion therefore has an in-currency part and an out-currency part that each balance on their own; no exchange-rate plug is ever needed, and nothing silently moves between the client's books and NPL's.
  3. Ownership is a tag on every line (ownership_tag client / company) and the account code says the same (CL. / CO.). Ownership moves at named events only (invariant 16). Client → company: margin recognition at conversion; the client side of an earnings offset; a forex true-up gain on an invoice line; the client's share of a loss. Company → client: a fee NPL absorbs or covers, a round-up, a shortfall top-up, a bounce make-whole, a loss make-good; the company side of an earnings offset; a forex true-up loss. Each transaction balances per owner, so these movements are always explicit lines.
  4. A cost component on every line (cost_component): principal, earnings, rebate, partner_cost, bank_fee, network_fee, variance, share, rounding, fx_timing, cutoff_timing, loss, and resale for the reseller project. Reports of margin, cost and variance are sums over this tag; no figure is stored.
  5. Accruals post with the conversion. NPL's margin, each markup-share party's part and the partner rebate expected on the leg are all recognised at conversion, as separate transactions that name the same deal; the monthly statement settles them and any difference is a true-up, never a rewrite.
  6. Variances are booked by kind, to four accounts: VAR_CONVERSION (the partner did something other than expected), VAR_CUTOFF (D29), VAR_RATE_HONOUR (D45), VAR_FX_TIMING (D24 and the own desk). Each can be a gain or a loss; each line names the leg or line it arose on.
  7. A loss is NPL's first. A LOSS_EVENT posts the whole amount to EXP_LOSS and makes the client whole in the same transaction; each share the partner, the sender or the client agrees to bear is a separate, later recovery posting.
  8. NPL-GR is the client of its own remittance project. Its reseller books are two postings per deal in the invoice currency, one per invoice, and a claim on the remittance side; the remittance deal posts like any client project; the forex result of paying the vendor in instalments is a true-up to VAR_FX_TIMING against NPL's pool, so the reseller revenue is never diluted.
  9. Earnings may be kept in the collection currency and offset between partners. A conversion may keep the deal's margin unconverted (residual_in) and an approved EARNINGS_OFFSET may swap NPL's margin at one partner for the same value of unconverted client money at another, at a stated rate; the client's entitlement never changes, and NPL's exchange of one currency for another is recorded in FX_RESULT (Pattern N).
  10. The views are sums over postings. PROJECT_BALANCE is the balance of the DUE account per partner; GROUP_ENTITLEMENT is the sum of PAYABLE postings by receiver_group_id, which every promise line carries; neither is computed from deals (D46 amendment).
  11. Append-only. A correction is a reversing transaction that names what it reverses (reversal_of_id) followed by the correct posting (invariant 2).
  12. Amounts stay in their own currency. A converted total (volumes, earnings, balances, custody in the reporting currency) is a REPORTING_VALUE: computed at read time from the latest platform-pair market rate, carrying that rate and its time, flagged when stale, and never posted, quoted or settled (invariant 38).
  13. Sent is discharged. A settlement line discharges the promise when the partner releases the money; the receiver's confirmation posts nothing, a short delivery re-raises the promise for the missing part, and a return re-raises it for what came back. What is sent and unconfirmed is a line state, not a jar.
  14. Rounding has an owner. Bank transfers go out exact, cents included (amount_rounding exact); rounding to a unit applies where the pair says so, in practice to cash hand-overs. A dropped fraction stays client money in the balance; an amount added by rounding up is NPL's cost in EXP_ROUNDING (invariant 24).

3 · Chart of accounts

Account codes read OWNER.HOLDER.CURRENCY.PURPOSE. The owner is CL for client funds or CO for company. The holder says where the money is or whom it concerns: a partner code such as ALI, JETON or AQN (a PARTNER_CONFIG, so always within one project), OWNDESK for the own-desk configuration of the reseller project, OWN for NPL's own wallets, a receiver, a sender, a markup-share party, PROJECT for the project as a whole, or NONE for income, expense and clearing accounts. One account exists per combination actually used; accounts are created on first posting (LEDGER_ACCOUNT: code, ownership, holder_type, holder_id, currency, purpose, kind). Purposes marked v5.7 (D46) entered the ERD with this edition; Section 24 lists the decision.

5 · Reading a posting table

Each worked example shows one transaction as a table. Debit and Credit are the amounts in bookkeeping's two columns; In / Out says the same thing in one word: every transaction moves value from somewhere to somewhere, a debit marks the account the value goes to (IN) and a credit the account it comes from (OUT). The word after the dot says what kind of account it is. money is a place where money sits (a partner, a wallet, a balance): IN means money arrives there, OUT that it leaves. promise is what NPL has undertaken to deliver: OUT means NPL now holds money on the strength of a promise (the promise is the source), IN means the promise is being fulfilled. earned is NPL's margin or income: OUT means earnings are the source of money that lands somewhere, usually NPL's pool at a partner. cost is what NPL bears: IN means value goes to a cost. A money or cost account is full when more has gone in than out; a promise or earned account is full when more has come out of it than gone in, because those two are sources by nature; the balance tables show the right figure either way. A reader who ignores the Debit and Credit columns and reads only Account, In / Out and Why will follow every example. Owner is the ownership tag; Cost component is the breakdown tag; Why says in plain words what that line records. The running example is project Evo: Sender A, Receiver X (receiver group A, the default group) with entities Entity X1 and Entity X2; partner Ali (fee_in_rate: its cost is inside its rate), partner Jeton (fee_on_market + 1.25 %, Evo's Jeton fee) and partner Aquanow (fee_on_market + 0.80 %, rebate 0.3 % of the amount). Rates: market 0.9000 EUR per USDT, Ali 0.8910 (Ali's cost is assumed at 1 % inside its rate; the real figure changes daily). Fee structure: rate_basis market (the sender is priced on the market rate and Ali converts at its own), fixed, 2.00 % in total: this sender type pays 1.40 % (Evo's other type pays 1.00 %) and the receiver always pays 0.60 %. A settlement drains a partner's holding into one or many lines; each line is confirmed by its receiver in full or short. The bank-fee treatment of the project or of a FEE_DECISION decides who bears a fee the bank took.

12 · Pattern G — inter-group offsets

An offset is not an account. It arises only when a counterparty receiver of one group is paid from a leg attributed to another group (invariant 17): the settlement posts exactly as T7 and T8, and the OFFSET view, reading GROUP_ENTITLEMENT, shows one group owed and the other holding its flow. A project with a single group never has an offset; a cross-group payout needs Management approval (cross_group_payout) and tighter aging. No ledger line differs, which is the point: the money and the claim are right, only the attribution needs watching.

18 · Pattern M — the reseller project (NPL-GR): two invoices, one remittance deal

NPL-GR, a business unit of NPL, buys from vendors at a preferential price and sells to its customers at a retail price (D23, D24). There are two invoices per deal: the vendor's invoice to NPL-GR, and NPL-GR's resale invoice to its customer, both in the vendor's currency. The difference is the game reseller revenue, and it must stay intact whatever happens afterwards. The customer pays the resale invoice through the remittance service with NPL's remittance markup on top; that markup pays for the remittance work, covers the partner's cost and absorbs the forex result of paying the vendor in instalments at different days' rates, and what is left is remittance earnings (NPL, 10 October).

The ledger therefore keeps three things apart. The reseller books are two postings per deal in the invoice currency, one per invoice, plus a claim on the remittance side. The remittance deal posts exactly like any client project, with NPL-GR as the client (holder PROJECT = the NPL-GR remittance project) and the resale invoice as the obligation the deal group pays. The forex result of each discharging line is a true-up between NPL-GR's USDT balance and NPL's pool, booked to VAR_FX_TIMING. The example uses NPL's own figures: a 9,000 EUR vendor invoice resold at 9,450 EUR, collected in THB, paid to the vendor in USDT in two instalments, the first at the group's invoice_rate (1.148369 USDT per EUR, 0.8708 the other way) and the second at 1.142505 (0.87527). 19 · Pattern N — earnings kept in the unconverted balance and offset between partners

Partners convert everything they receive, so NPL's margin is converted along with the client's money and rests at the partner in the payout currency, invisible to the partner (EARNINGS_RECEIVABLE, Pattern A). NPL's practice (10 October) is to take its earnings out of collections that are not yet converted instead, and to offset between partners: where Jeton has converted a large collection in full, and Ali holds a collection that converts tomorrow, NPL leaves in Ali's USDT balance the USDT equivalent of its margin at Jeton and of its margin on the Ali deal, and converts only the rest. Jeton's EUR balance is then entirely the client's; at Ali the USDT balance is NPL's and the EUR balance the client's.

In the ledger this is two things. Retention is a conversion that keeps the deal's own margin in the collection currency (CONVERSION.residual_in), so EARN_GROSS is recognised in USDT rather than EUR. The offset is an exchange at the day's rate: NPL's EUR at one partner becomes client money there, the same value of the client's unconverted USDT at another partner becomes NPL's, and the client's claim is re-denominated accordingly. For the client it is a conversion NPL performed out of its own earnings; for NPL it is a currency swap, recorded in FX_RESULT. Neither moves money physically, and neither changes any client's entitlement.

Where NPL later converts its USDT pool at Ali into EUR, that is another exchange of company money, posted to FX_RESULT in the same way; the reporting-currency value of FX_RESULT over the period is NPL's currency result.

21 · Reports the ledger answers

  • Where the money is. Client money by partner, in transit, in custody (CUSTODY view: reroutes, bridge, own desk), shortfalls and credits; NPL's money by partner (POOL) and wallet (WALLET), receivables and payables by counterparty.
  • Client position. PROJECT_BALANCE per partner and GROUP_ENTITLEMENT per group, rolled up per client (CLIENT_POSITION) with the period's EARN_GROSS and EARN_REBATE; compared with the client's exposure ceiling as a REPORTING_VALUE.
  • Profit and loss, per currency. EARN_GROSS (net of shares) + EARN_REBATE + RESELL_REVENUE − RESELL_COST − EXP_PARTNER − EXP_BANKFEE − EXP_NETWORK − EXP_ROUNDING − EXP_LOSS ± the four VAR_ accounts, by project, partner, pair and month, from the cost-component and source tags. A total across currencies is a REPORTING_VALUE with its rate and time.
  • Partner commissions. Per partner and period: margin captured there, dues outstanding (EARNINGS_RECEIVABLE), rebates expected, reconciled and received (statement_ref).
  • Markup shares. Per party and period: base, share, direction, settled and open amounts; a party never sees a rebate.
  • Variance and loss. The four variance kinds by partner and month, each line traceable to its leg; loss events with their shares and recovery state.
  • Reseller and remittance margins, kept apart, and NPL's profit. Per resale invoice: revenue, cost and game reseller revenue in EUR, intact (RESELLER_MARGIN). Per NPL-GR deal: remittance earnings, partner cost, forex result and fees in the converted currency (REMITTANCE_MARGIN). 23 · Who sees what, and who may post

Rights are rows, not text (D44): ROLE holds the three roles of today, operations, finance and management, and ROLE_RIGHT says per controlled action which role may initiate and which may approve, with the role added on escalation; the approver is never the initiator (invariant 9).

  • Operations (sees_economics false) sees operational fields, sender-facing prices and partner rates as entered, but never a ledger balance, a margin, a pool, a share, a rebate, a cost breakdown or a variance, on any screen, export, message or report.
  • Finance sees all postings and balances, owns reconciliation and the month-end true-ups, initiates adjustments, balance conversions and dues recovery, recommends covered bank fees and fee caps (FEE_DECISION.recommended_by), approves honoured expired rates and quote-validity extensions, and overrides a screening reject.
  • Management approves the actions the ledger patterns depend on: approve_loss_split, balance_conversion, recover_dues, cross_group_payout, cover_bank_fee, raise_fee_cap, enable_own_desk, settlements above limit and past the cutoff, own-wallet outbound transfers, and post-money voids. Every controlled action is audited with actor, time, reason and record version.

24 · What this design put into the ERD (D46 in v5.7, amended as v5.8)

The patterns above required the following additions, agreed with NPL on 10–11 October and cut into ERD Draft v5.7 as decision D46. Recovery postings use the source LossRecovery; the reseller books the source Invoice; the offset the source EarningsOffset. An offset records the to-partner's rate of the day (NPL's choice over the market rate), and the reseller books are postings rather than invoice records with views, so that NPL's profit (NPL_PROFIT) can be asked for as remittance earnings, reseller earnings, or both. An independent re-derivation of every calculation from the ERD and this document, run on 11 October, found five gaps between the model's definitions and these postings; they were applied the same evening as an amendment of D46, cut as ERD v5.8, and are marked below.

  1. Variance by kind. LEDGER_ACCOUNT.purpose: VARIANCE replaced by VAR_CONVERSION, VAR_CUTOFF, VAR_RATE_HONOUR, VAR_FX_TIMING. Invariants 28 and 39 and decisions D24, D29, D45 then name the account they post to.
  2. Loss first, recover later. Purposes LOSS_RECEIVABLE (holder Partner or Sender), and the pair MAKEGOOD (client asset, holder Project) / LOSS_PAYABLE (company liability) for an unfunded make-good. A table LOSS_RECOVERY (loss_event_id, party kind sender / partner / client, amount, state agreed / received, received_at, reference, approved_by) as the source record of each recovery posting, under source LossEvent. COLLECTION.state reversed is used only when the deal is unwound. Invariant 16 restated (amendment) as two lists — client → company: margin recognition, the client side of an offset, a forex true-up gain, the client's loss share; company → client: absorbed or covered fees, round-ups, top-ups, make-wholes, make-goods, the company side of an offset, a forex true-up loss.
  3. Accrual timing. MARKUP_SHARE_ACCRUAL and PARTNER_REBATE_ACCRUAL are created and posted by the conversion that gives rise to them (invariant 25 and 26 restated to say so); PARTNER_REBATE_RULE.payout_currency names the currency the partner pays in, in which the accrual is expressed. Amendment: basis keeps pct_of_amount only; pct_of_partner_fee, retired with the D27 amendment, is dropped.
  4. Reseller project. INVOICE.kind vendor / resale, with receiver_id for the vendor and sender_id for the customer of a resale invoice; DEAL_GROUP.invoice_id names the resale invoice the deal collects; SETTLEMENT_LINE.invoice_id names the invoice a line discharges (vendor invoice for a vendor line, resale invoice for the margin line), with obligation_discharged and discharge_rate (payout currency per invoice currency) as today; purposes RESELL_REVENUE, RESELL_COST, VENDOR_PAYABLE, RESALE_RECEIVABLE, REMIT_CLAIM; source Invoice; cost component resale; views RESELLER_MARGIN(resale invoice) = resale − vendor amount and REMITTANCE_MARGIN(deal) = earnings − partner cost ± fx timing − fees. D24's forex line is the true-up of T50 and T51; D23 stands: own-desk balances are ordinary partner-coded client accounts with the own-desk configuration as holder. Amendment: INVOICE.vendor_invoice_id pairs a resale invoice with the vendor invoice it marks up; DEAL_GROUP.invoice_amount (the part of the resale invoice this group collects, advance then balance) and invoice_rate (payout currency per invoice currency at pricing, the stored base of every reserved amount); INVOICE_BALANCE is a receivable balance for a resale invoice (amount − Σ invoice_amount collected) and a payable balance over released lines for a vendor invoice; invariant 23 restated for the two-invoice case; invariant 44 adds that a cash-rounding surplus is revenue, not a forex result.
  5. Earnings retention and offset. CONVERSION.residual_in (already in the model) is the margin kept in the collection currency, with EARN_GROSS recognised in that currency; a table EARNINGS_OFFSET (project, from partner config and currency and amount, to partner config and currency and amount, the market rate or partner rate version used, the EARNINGS_RECEIVABLE rows recovered, requested_by Finance, approved_by Management, state) as the source of an EarningsOffset transaction; EARNINGS_RECEIVABLE.recovered_by_offset_id beside recovered_by_deal_id; ROLE_RIGHT action earnings_offset. Invariant 16 adds the offset as a movement of ownership in both directions at one stated rate.
  6. NPL's own currency exchanges. Purpose FX_RESULT (holder None; amendment, replacing FX_CLEARING): a profit-and-loss account per currency — currency NPL sold is a debit, currency it bought a credit — for an earnings offset, a pool squared from NPL's USDT, a rebate, share or loss share paid in another currency, or NPL-GR's EUR claim paid to its wallet in USDT. Its reporting-currency value is NPL's realised currency result; it needs no closing. (FX_CLEARING, an asset, could never be closed under per-currency balancing, since only an income or expense line in each currency can absorb it.)
  7. Views over postings. LEDGER_POSTING.receiver_group_id on every PAYABLE line; PROJECT_BALANCE = the DUE account balance per partner; GROUP_ENTITLEMENT = Σ PAYABLE postings by group; invariant 7 restated over postings (amendment: the v5.3 definitions summed deals and no longer agreed with the accounts).
  8. Payout mechanics. INTRANSIT retired for client money: the promise is discharged on release (SETTLEMENT released_at), re-raised by SETTLEMENT_RETURN or a short CONFIRMATION; invariant 7 already subtracts sent lines from the entitlement, so the ledger now matches it. CURRENCY_PAIR.amount_rounding gains the value exact (the default for bank transfers; the unit options apply to cash hand-overs). A pool may be negative between a top-up NPL funded and the next conversion, which then includes NPL's own USDT (a conversion with a company part, posted through FX_RESULT).
  9. Fee arithmetic. Invariant 42: fee parts are percentages of the principal at the basis rate, never compounded; amendment: in amount_mode target_out the fee is a markup on the target — amount_in = target × (1 + sender_pays_pct) ÷ rate, client net = target × (1 − receiver_pays_pct) plus any cash-rounding surplus — NPL's own quoting convention, which the ERD had left unstated.
  10. Holder types. LEDGER_ACCOUNT.holder_type keeps Sender for LOSS_RECEIVABLE and RESALE_RECEIVABLE and Receiver for VENDOR_PAYABLE; the own desk is a PARTNER_CONFIG, not a new type.
