1 · Three roles, one principle

Every controlled action is maker-checker: one person initiates, a different person in the approving role approves, and the system refuses an approval by the initiator. Since the rights are configuration, not code: ROLE holds the three roles of today and ROLE_RIGHT says per controlled action which role may initiate, which may approve and which is added on escalation; the approver's role must hold may_approve for the action. Visibility is cumulative: Operations sees the least, Finance sees everything Operations sees plus the economics, Management sees everything.

2 · The deal: Quote → Collect → Convert → Settle

A deal is a group of one or more legs. A quote-first leg walks the whole top row of Figure 1; a collect-first leg is born at Collected because the money arrived before any quote; an estimate-first leg enters Collecting with an indicative figure and is priced at conversion; a return leg is born at Collected, funded by the returned payout and attributed to its parent leg's group. Pricing fields are stamped once and never recomputed. Fee parts are percentages of the principal at the basis rate and never compound; a target-out quote applies the fee as a markup on the target.

4 · Losses, offsets and the reseller's invoices

NPL-GR's two invoices

In the reseller project NPL-GR is the client of its own remittance deal. The vendor's invoice to NPL-GR and NPL-GR's resale invoice to its customer are posted once each, in the invoice currency, when received and when issued; the resale invoice names the vendor invoice it marks up. A deal group that collects the resale invoice names it and the part it collects (advance, then balance) at the pricing rate; each vendor payment line names the vendor invoice, the obligation it discharges and the rate at discharge, and the forex result of paying on a later day's rate is a true-up on the remittance side, never on the reseller revenue. A resale invoice is settled when its groups have collected its amount; a vendor invoice when its released lines have discharged its amount (INVOICE_BALANCE by kind).

6 · Rejection handling, the same way everywhere

  1. Nothing is deleted. The request, the rejection and its reason stay on the APPROVAL record and in the audit log. A re-submission is a new approval request that points at the rejected one.
  2. The record returns to the initiator's last safe state. A rejected quote is not sent; a rejected settlement goes back to Prepared; a rejected reroute leaves the route unchanged; a rejected balance conversion leaves the balance in its currency; a rejected offset leaves every margin where it rests.
  3. Money that already moved is never rolled back by a rejection. A conversion the partner has booked stands; a variance exception is resolved by a decision and, if needed, a reversing ledger entry, not by editing the conversion. A loss that Management has approved stands as NPL's; only its shares are recovered.
  4. A rejection reason is mandatory and is visible to the initiator; derived-economics fields are stripped from the reason shown to Operations.
  5. Frozen states need a decision, not time. A breached rate version, an open variance exception, an open offset, custody in NPL's wallet, an unfunded make-good and a negative pool all stay on the daily exception list until a role with authority acts.
  6. Escalation never skips a level. Management approves in addition to Finance; when only one Finance user is on shift, Management's countersignature stands in for the second Finance user.

8 · Aging and the daily exception list

These things age and are reported daily with deal, reason and hours since the event: client money in NPL's own wallet (custody — reroutes, bridge collections awaiting their hop, own-desk balances), open offsets across receiver groups, open shortfalls, open variance exceptions, a pool below zero awaiting the next conversion, an unfunded make-good (MAKEGOOD / LOSS_PAYABLE), loss recoveries agreed and not received, and settlement lines sent beyond the rail's expected time. Breached rate versions, frozen partner × pairs and quotes past their validity appear on the same list. Time limits for each are project configuration (THRESHOLD, OWN_WALLET.custody_max_hours) and are not fixed by this document.
