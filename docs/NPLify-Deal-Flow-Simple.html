How an NPLify deal flows

Operations starts each step and Finance approves it, Operations starts each step and Finance approves the controlled actions where the Lifecycle & Approvals document says so (never the person who initiated); money passes from sender to partner to receiver, and exceptions and the daily close hang off the side of the deal.
