Level 0The business and the system

NPL coordinates collections, conversions and settlements across a network of remittance partners on behalf of its clients, on fiat and USDT rails. NPLify is the internal, configuration-driven platform that runs every project of that business as configuration rather than code. Read this level left to right: who pays, who runs it, who converts and pays out.

Clients and projects

  • Betfair — BF, BF Sub, BF Withdrawal (B.1–B.3)
  • Evo — invoice payments into EUR; dormant currency products kept in scope (B.4)
  • NPL-GR — Game Reseller, NPL's own business unit as the client, own desk (B.5)
  • YN Remit, 66 Group, GDC, Peru — invoice-payment projects (B.6–B.9)
  • JTN, Jonas — cash projects (B.10–B.11)
  • AK Personal — balance held at the partner (B.12)
  • Kingdom, Working Lab — the invoice-payment pattern (B.13)

6 client groups · 13 SOPs · 27 scenarios. Each project is one arrangement with a client; senders, receiver groups and receiving entities belong to the project and are partner-independent.

NPL runs the deal

Quote→Collect→Convert→Settle

Every deal is a group of one or more legs; pricing is stamped once; NPL's margin is captured at conversion; release discharges the promise. Every controlled action is maker-checker.

OperationsQuotes, screens wallets, verifies collections, instructs conversions, prepares settlements, requests reroutes. Never sees the economics.
FinanceApproves what Operations initiates, confirms the day's rates, resolves exceptions, reconciles daily and at month end, proposes configuration.
ManagementApproves escalations and the heavier actions: loss splits, offsets, balance conversions, dues recovery, covered fees, own-wallet transfers, post-money voids, configuration, users.

The system: one Next.js / TypeScript service (screens, API, background jobs), one PostgreSQL database whose ledger and audit tables accept inserts only, one S3 bucket for evidence, in NPL's own AWS account in Hong Kong (two application tasks in two zones and a standby database in a second zone, for recovery within one hour; a live standby database, backups, log and bucket in a second region, so that the same hour holds for a whole-region failure). Four outside services, none trusted with the books: Kraken and CoinMarketCap for market rates, Tronscan and Etherscan for transaction hashes, one Google Sheet per client as its status journal.

Partners and rails

  • Ali — SWIFT via PT Sukses / PT Global / PT Silver Lining; cash by token in named cities; one-time local accounts; 14:30 Hong Kong bank cutoff; cost inside its rate
  • Jeton — USDT collections and bank-in for withdrawals; fee on market 1–1.25 %; rebate 0.4 % monthly; no cutoff
  • Aquanow — USDT → EUR with internal transfer to BF; fee 0.8 %; rebate 0.3 %; no cutoff
  • NPL's own desk and wallets — LT Sub at Aquanow; reroute contingency; bridge on a network a partner lacks

Rails: USDT on TRC-20 / ERC-20 with wallet screening · SWIFT / SEPA · cash by token · local deposit and local payout · internal transfer inside the partner's platform. Ten shared flows F1–F10 cover them all.

The other state machines

Settlement (Figure 2)

Prepared (Ops) → Approved (Finance) → Released by partner (execution_due_date) → Completed when every line is confirmed, short or returned. Rejected → back to Prepared. Approved after the partner's cutoff → next business day. Line: sent (promise discharged) → confirmed (no posting) | short (SHORTFALL opens, topped up next run) | returned (promise re-raised; funds back in balance or fund a return leg). Management approves when: above settlement limit · third same-day slot · counterparty paid from another group's leg · only one Finance user on shift (countersign).

Partner rate version (Figure 3)

Active (new version) → Usable when spread within avg_2w × (1 + threshold) → Expired at expires_at. Spread above limit → Breached (pair frozen) → Finance approves with reason → Approved → quoting resumes; or Rejected / better rate requested (a new version). No skip path.

Reroute (Figure 4)

Requested (Ops, trigger) → Approved (Finance) → In custody (collection lands in own wallet) → Forwarded to alternate partner (outbound: Finance + Management, signers) → Completed (alternate partner converts; margin captured there). Rejected → route unchanged. forward_and_recover_dues: receivable ↓, wallet ↑ → Dues recovered (Management).

Approval (Figure 5)

Requested (initiator) → Approved (approving role, different user) → Executed (action runs; audit written). Limit or rule requires Management → Escalated to Management → Management approves / rejects. Approver rejects → Rejected (reason). Nothing is deleted.

Loss event (Figure 6)

Loss recorded (Ops); split proposed (Finance) → Approved (Management, approve_loss_split) → Posted: loss NPL's (EXP_LOSS), client made whole from pool / wallet (else MAKEGOOD / LOSS_PAYABLE until NPL's funds land) → Recovery agreed per share (LOSS_RECEIVABLE) → Recovery received. Management may decide to unwind: the collection part is reversed.

Earnings offset (Figure 7)

Requested (Finance) → Approved (Management) → Posted: FX_RESULT records the swap; EARNINGS_RECEIVABLE rows marked recovered_by_offset_id. Rejected → margins stay put.
